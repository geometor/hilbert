§ 39: Criterion for the Possibility of a Geometrical Construction by Means of a Straight-Edge and a Transferer of Segments — Summary
====================================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Mathematical Overview — *§ 39: Kriterium für die Ausführbarkeit geometrischer Konstruktionen mittelst Lineals und Streckenübertragers*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 39 — Geometric Multiplicity Criterion (Theorem 44)**: Combines `Theorems 41–43` to establish a purely geometric necessary and sufficient criterion (`Theorem 44`, proved for 1-parameter problems): a ruler-and-compass problem requiring :math:`n` minimal square roots is solvable by straightedge and segment-transferer if and only if it possesses **exactly** :math:`2^n` **real solutions** for all real parameter positions of the given points! Concludes that all Gauss-constructible regular polygons and Malfatti's problem are solvable with straightedge and segment-transferer, whereas Apollonius's circle-tangency problem is not.
