GEOMETOR • hilbert
==================

.. rubric:: Foundations of Geometry & Metamathematical Works of David Hilbert (1862–1943)

Book Codex
----------

:doc:`Foundations of Geometry — Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899 / 1902) <grundlagen/index>`
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*Festschrift zur Feier der Enthüllung des Gauss-Weber-Denkmals in Göttingen*, Erster Theil (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, June 1899) · Authorized English translation by **E. J. Townsend**, Ph.D. (Chicago: The Open Court Publishing Company, 1902).

Hilbert's epoch-making axiomatic reconstruction of Euclidean, non-Euclidean, non-Archimedean, non-Desarguesian, and non-Pascalian geometries—uniting synthetic incidence, order, parallels, congruence, and continuity with algebraic number domains (:math:`\Omega`, :math:`\Omega(t)`, :math:`\Omega(s, t)`), segment arithmetic (*Streckenrechnung*), polygonal area theory, and straightedge-and-gauge constructibility.
