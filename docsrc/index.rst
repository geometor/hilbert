=============
David Hilbert
=============

.. image:: _static/medallion.svg
   :alt: David Hilbert Medallion
   :class: repo-medallion-hero

:person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>` (1862–1943) inaugurated modern axiomatic geometry in 1899 by stripping away unstated spatial assumptions and rebuilding Euclidean and non-Euclidean geometry from five explicit axiom groups—proving their consistency, mutual independence, and structural connection to algebraic coordinate fields. This synchronized four-track German–English edition pairs the 1899 Leipzig *Festschrift* with Edgar Jerome Townsend's 1902 authorized English translation, calibrated Teubner diagrams, and cross-corpus concordances.

Key Features
------------

.. container:: home-feature-grid

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Axiomatic Independence & Models**

      Explore Hilbert's Group I–V axioms alongside explicit non-Euclidean, non-Archimedean, non-Pascalian, and non-Desarguesian independence models in :doc:`Chapter II <grundlagen/chapter_02/index>`.

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **1899/1902 vs. 1930 Concordance**

      The :doc:`Master Concordance <grundlagen/concordance>` maps every section and axiom-numbering shift between the 1899/1902 editions and the 7th German edition (1930), linking Euclid, Hoenen, and Pappus.

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Eleven Calibrated Vector Diagrams**

      Inspect interactive reconstructions of Pasch's betweenness axiom, segment multiplication, Desargues's configuration, and gauge constructions across all four reading tracks.
