§ 37: Analytical Representation of the Co-ordinates of Points Which Can Be So Constructed — Summary
===================================================================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Mathematical Overview — *§ 37: Analytische Darstellung der Coordinaten konstruirbarer Punkte*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 37 — Pythagorean Radical Criterion (Theorem 41)**: Proves that a construction problem is solvable by straightedge and segment-transferer if and only if the coordinates of the sought points belong to the domain :math:`\Omega(R)` generated from the given coordinates :math:`R` by :math:`+, -, \times, \div` and :math:`\sqrt{\alpha^2 + \beta^2}` (`Theorem 41`). Exhibits an explicit compass-constructible right triangle (hypotenuse :math:`1`, leg :math:`|\sqrt{2}| - 1`, second leg :math:`\sqrt{2|\sqrt{2}| - 2}`) **impossible** with straightedge and segment-transferer because its conjugate :math:`\sqrt{-2|\sqrt{2}| - 2}` is imaginary, whereas every number in :math:`\Omega` is totally real!
