Chapter II: The Compatibility and Mutual Independence of the Axioms — Summary
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: *Kapitel II: Die Widerspruchslosigkeit und gegenseitige Unabhängigkeit der Axiome* — David Hilbert (1899 / 1902) — §§ 9–12

Having erected the five axiom groups in Chapter I, :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>` inaugurates **model-theoretic metamathematics** in Chapter II, establishing both :term:`compatibility <widerspruchslosigkeit>` (*Widerspruchslosigkeit*) and mutual :term:`independence <unabhaengigkeit>` (*Unabhängigkeit*) through four explicit analytic coordinate models:

* **§ 9 — Relative Consistency via the Pythagorean Field** :math:`\Omega`: Constructs the countable algebraic number domain :math:`\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}` generated from :math:`1` by finite applications of :math:`+, -, \times, \div` and the fifth operation :math:`\sqrt{1 + \omega^2}`. Defining points as :math:`(x, y) \in \Omega^2`, lines as homogeneous ratios :math:`(u : v : w)`, order via algebraic inequalities, and displacements via rational/Pythagorean orthogonal rotations, :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>` proves that every contradiction deducible from Axioms `I–V` would already appear in the arithmetic of :math:`\Omega`.
* **§ 10 — Independence of the Parallel Axiom III**: Proves first that existence of parallels follows from `I, II, IV` alone (via alternate angles, `Euclid I.27`), and then restricts the :math:`\Omega`-space of § 9 to the interior of a fixed sphere equipped with projective Cayley–:person:`Klein <klein>` collineations preserving the sphere, yielding a consistent :person:`Bolyai <bolyai>`–:person:`Lobachevsky <lobachevsky>` hyperbolic geometry in which `I, II, IV, V` hold while `III` fails.
* **§ 11 — Independence of Congruence Axiom IV 6 (SAS)**: Retains ordinary points, lines, planes, and angles, but redefines segment length between :math:`(x_1, y_1, z_1)` and :math:`(x_2, y_2, z_2)` by the anisotropic norm

  .. math::

     \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2 + y_1 - y_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2 + (z_1 - z_2)^2}.

  Linear parameter proportionality preserves segment additivity (`IV 3`), so `I, II, III, IV 1–5, V` all hold, yet right triangles :math:`OAC` and :math:`OBC` with :math:`O(0,0), A(1,0), B(0,1), C(\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2})` share :math:`\angle C`, :math:`OC`, and :math:`AC = BC = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}` while :math:`OA = 1 \neq \sqrt{2} = OB`, falsifying SAS (`Theorem 10` and `IV 6`).
* **§ 12 — Independence of Archimedes's Axiom V**: Replaces numbers by the countable field :math:`\Omega(t)` of real algebraic functions of a parameter :math:`t` generated by :math:`+, -, \times, \div` and :math:`\sqrt{1 + \omega^2}`, ordered by sign for sufficiently large :math:`t > 0`. Since :math:`n - t < 0` for every positive integer :math:`n` as :math:`t \to +\infty`, the segment :math:`1` laid off :math:`n` times never reaches :math:`t`, establishing a consistent :term:`non-Archimedean geometry <nicht-archimedische-geometrie>` satisfying `I–IV` while violating `V`.
