Chapter V: Desargues's Theorem — Summary
========================================

.. rubric:: *Kapitel V: Der Desarguessche Satz* — David Hilbert (1899 / 1902) — §§ 22–30

Chapters V and VI pivot from metric Euclidean geometry to projective-affine foundations, investigating what happens when **Congruence (`IV`) is dropped altogether**:

* **§ 22 — Desargues's Theorem (Theorem 32)**: Formulates the affine :term:`Desargues's Theorem <desarguesscher-satz>` (`Theorem 32`) for perspective triangles with parallel sides, observing that it follows effortlessly either from 3D spatial incidence (`I 1–7, II, III`) or from 2D planar congruence (`I 1–2, II–IV` via Chapter III coordinates).
* **§ 23 — Hilbert's Non-Desarguesian Plane (Theorem 33)**: Constructs a celebrated planar countermodel inside the Euclidean :math:`XY`-plane: straight lines cutting the ellipse :math:`x^2 + 4y^2 = 1` have their interior chord replaced by the circular arc passing through the two boundary intersections :math:`P, Q` and the exterior point :math:`F(\tfrac{3}{2}, 0)` (since :math:`\tfrac{3}{2} > \tfrac{\sqrt{7}}{2}`, no circle meeting the ellipse four times can pass through :math:`F`). This deformed plane satisfies **all planar axioms `I 1–2, II, III, IV 1–5, V`** while violating Desargues's Theorem—proving planar Desargues unprovable without either 3D spatial incidence (`I 3–7`) or triangle congruence (`IV 6`)!
* **§§ 24–26 — Congruence-Free Segment Calculus**: Fixes two intersecting axes :math:`OE, OE'` and a unit line :math:`EE'` in any plane satisfying `I 1–2, II, III` + Desargues (`Theorem 32`), defining addition :math:`c = a + b` and multiplication :math:`c = ab` purely by parallel projections. Using intricate multi-triangle Desargues configurations (Figures 41–45, drafted with :person:`Hans von Schaper <schaper>`), :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>` proves commutative and associative addition (`§ 25`), associative multiplication :math:`a(bc) = (ab)c`, and both left and right distributive laws (`§ 26`)—**all without Congruence**!
* **§§ 27–30 — Skew-Field Coordinatization and 3D Embeddability (Theorems 34–35)**: Proves every line has a *left*-linear equation :math:`ax + by + c = 0` (`Theorem 34`, whereas :math:`xa + yb + c = 0` need not be a line when multiplication is non-commutative!), shows the segments form an ordered associative division ring (:term:`Desarguessches Zahlensystem <desarguessches-zahlensystem>`, rules `1–11, 13–16` of § 13), reconstructs 3D space over any Desarguesian division ring (`§ 29`), and crowns the chapter with `Theorem 35`: Desargues's Theorem is the **necessary and sufficient condition** for a planar geometry (`I 1–2, II, III`) to be embeddable as a plane inside a 3D space (`I, II, III`).
