§ 3: Group II: Axioms of Order
==============================

.. rubric:: David Hilbert — *The Foundations of Geometry* (Authorized Translation by E. J. Townsend, 1902)

The axioms of this group define the idea expressed by the word “between,” and make possible, upon the basis of this idea, an *order of sequence* of the points upon a straight line, in a plane, and in space. The points of a straight line have a certain relation to one another which the word “between” serves to describe. The axioms of this group are as follows:

**II, 1.** *If* :math:`A`, :math:`B`, :math:`C` *are points of a straight line and* :math:`B` *lies between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`C`, *then* :math:`B` *lies also between* :math:`C` *and* :math:`A`.

**II, 2.** *If* :math:`A` *and* :math:`C` *are two points of a straight line, then there exists at least one point* :math:`B` *lying between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`C` *and at least one point* :math:`D` *so situated that* :math:`C` *lies between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`D`.

**II, 3.** *Of any three points situated on a straight line, there is always one and only one which lies between the other two.*

**II, 4.** *Any four points* :math:`A`, :math:`B`, :math:`C`, :math:`D` *of a straight line can always be so arranged that* :math:`B` *shall lie between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`C` *and also between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`D`, *and, furthermore, that* :math:`C` *shall lie between* :math:`A` *and* :math:`D` *and also between* :math:`B` *and* :math:`D`.

**Definition**. We will call the system of two points :math:`A` and :math:`B`, lying upon a straight line, a *segment* and denote it by :math:`AB` or :math:`BA`. The points lying between :math:`A` and :math:`B` are called the *points of the segment* :math:`AB` or the *points lying within the segment* :math:`AB`. All other points of the straight line are referred to as the *points lying* *outside the segment* :math:`AB`. The points :math:`A` and :math:`B` are called the *extremities* of the segment :math:`AB`.

**II, 5.** *Let* :math:`A`, :math:`B`, :math:`C` *be three points not lying in the same straight line and let* :math:`a` *be a straight line lying in the plane* :math:`ABC` *and not passing through any of the points* :math:`A`, :math:`B`, :math:`C`. *Then, if the straight line* :math:`a` *passes through a point of the segment* :math:`AB`, *it will also pass through either a point of the segment* :math:`BC` *or a point of the segment* :math:`AC`. Axioms II, 1–4 contain statements concerning the points of a straight line only, and, hence, we will call them the *linear axioms of group II*. Axiom II, 5 relates to the elements of plane geometry and, consequently, shall be called the *plane axiom of group II*.
