Works (Opera)¶

Grundlagen der Geometrie — The Foundations of Geometry (1899 / 1902)¶

Festschrift zur Feier der Enthüllung des Gauss-Weber-Denkmals in Göttingen, Erster Theil (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, June 1899) · Authorized English translation by E. J. Townsend, Ph.D. (Chicago: The Open Court Publishing Company, 1902).

Hilbert’s epoch-making axiomatic reconstruction of Euclidean, non-Euclidean, non-Archimedean, non-Desarguesian, and non-Pascalian geometries—uniting synthetic incidence, order, parallels, congruence, and continuity with algebraic number domains ($\Omega$, $\Omega(t)$, $\Omega(s, t)$), segment arithmetic (Streckenrechnung), polygonal area theory, and straightedge-and-gauge constructibility.
