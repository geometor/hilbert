About

GEOMETOR hilbert reconstructs the axiomatic and metamathematical keystone of modern geometry: David Hilbert’s landmark treatise Grundlagen der Geometrie (The Foundations of Geometry). Published in June 1899 as Part I of the Festschrift zur Feier der Enthüllung des Gauss-Weber-Denkmals in Göttingen by B. G. Teubner, and authorized for English translation by Edgar Jerome Townsend in 1902 (The Open Court Publishing Company), Hilbert’s work established the modern axiomatic method. By detaching geometric entities from sensory intuition and establishing formal relation systems across five rigorous axiom groups (incidence, betweenness, congruence, parallels, and continuity), Hilbert demonstrated the independence, consistency, and structural limits of geometric deduction.
