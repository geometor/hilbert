2. Hilbert’s Twist Skew-Field $\Omega(s, t)$ (§ 33)¶

Hilbert’s § 33 construction (Theorem 39) is a tour de force of algebra: by forming iterated Laurent power series over two non-commuting indeterminates $s, t$ subject to

$$ts = 2st,$$

he created what algebraists today call a Hilbert–Mal’cev–Neumann twisted Laurent series division ring. Every nonzero element $S = s^m T_0 + s^{m+1} T_1 + \cdots$ ($T_0 \neq 0$) is invertible by recursive power-series comparison because $T_0$ lies in the commutative Laurent field $\mathbb{Q}((t))$ and conjugation $T(t) s = s T(2t)$ merely rescales $t \mapsto 2t$, preserving positivity of the leading rational coefficient $r_0$! Thus $\Omega(s, t)$ is simultaneously ordered, non-Archimedean, and non-commutative, giving birth to consistent 3D Non-Pascalian Geometry.
