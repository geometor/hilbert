Architectural Summary of Chapter VI (§§ 31–35)¶

Chapter VI brings the projective-algebraic hierarchy to its climax by isolating the exact axiomatic and algebraic meaning of Pascal's (Pappus's) Theorem when Congruence (`IV`) is omitted:

  • § 31 — Two Fundamental Theorems on Pappus/Pascal (Theorems 36–37): Announces two complementary results in 3D space without Congruence: 1. Theorem 36: Pappus/Pascal is provable from I, II, III, V (i.e., with Archimedes’s Axiom, even without Congruence!). 2. Theorem 37: Pappus/Pascal is unprovable from I, II, III alone (when both Congruence IV and Archimedes V are dropped).

  • § 32 — Hilbert’s Commutativity Theorem for Archimedean Skew-Fields (Theorem 38): Proves a landmark theorem of pure algebra: every Archimedean ordered division ring is necessarily commutative! Assuming $a > 0, b > 0$, and $ab - ba > 0$, Hilbert traps $a$ and $b$ between consecutive multiples $md < a < (m+1)d$ and $nd < b < (n+1)d$ using Archimedes’s Axiom (17) and shows $ab - ba < (m + n + 1)d^2 < (a + b + 1)c = ab - ba$, an outright contradiction!

  • § 33 — Hilbert’s Non-Commutative Twist Laurent Skew-Field $\Omega(s, t)$ (Theorem 39): Constructs the first explicit example in mathematics of an ordered non-Archimedean skew-field (Theorem 39): formal Laurent series $S = s^m T_0 + s^{m+1} T_1 + \cdots$ whose coefficients $T_k = r_0 t^n + r_1 t^{n+1} + \cdots$ are formal Laurent series in $t$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, governed by the non-commutative twist relation

    $$ts = 2st,$$

    and ordered lexicographically by the leading rational coefficient $r_0$ of $T_0$.

  • §§ 34–35 — Non-Pascalian Geometry and Intersection Completeness: Building 3-space over $\Omega(s, t)$ via § 29 immediately proves Theorem 37, exhibiting a consistent 3D Non-Pascalian Geometry (which by Theorem 36 is necessarily non-Archimedean). Conversely, § 35 proves that every planar incidence/intersection theorem holding under I–V reduces synthetically to a finite combination of Desargues’s and Pascal’s (Pappus’s) theorems without needing Congruence!
