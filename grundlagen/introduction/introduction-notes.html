GEOMETOR Commentary & Cross-Repository Notes¶

1. Kant’s Epigraph and the Triadic Architecture of the Grundlagen¶

Why does the founding manifesto of formal axiomatics open with Immanuel Kant’s dictum from the Transcendental Dialectic (Kritik der reinen Vernunft, A 702 / B 730)? Far from an ornamental flourish, Hilbert maps Kant’s three faculties onto the three stages of his treatise:

  • Anschauungen (*Intuitions*): The five pre-formal experiential clusters of spatial intuition—incidence, betweenness, parallelism, rigid congruence, and continuity—from which geometry historically arises.

  • Begriffe (*Concepts*): Chapter I’s relational axiomatization (§§ 1–8), where primitive geometric terms (Punkt, Gerade, Ebene) are stripped of sensory content and implicitly defined purely through logical axioms.

  • Ideen (*Regulative Ideas*): Chapters II–VII’s metamathematical investigations into consistency (Widerspruchslosigkeit), mutual independence (Unabhängigkeit), completeness (Vollständigkeit), and purity of method.

2. Hoenen’s Epistemological Critique (De Noetica Geometriae, Caput I & V)¶

In De Noetica Geometriae (Caput I §§ 5–6 and Caput V § 1), Fr. Peter Hoenen, S.J. places Hilbert’s Einleitung at the center of a three-way comparison between:

  1. Aristotle and Euclid (Posterior Analytics): Science (episteme) requires demonstration from true, necessary, and intelligible first principles grasped by intellectual insight (nous) through formal abstraction from the imaginative diagram (phantasma).

  2. Freudenthal’s Semi-Axiomatics (Freudenthal 1957): A prior “pre-geometry” (la doctrine préalable / noetics) establishes a complete set of intuitively true axioms at the outset, after which deduction must proceed blindly without further appeal to the diagram.

  3. Hilbert’s Strict Formal Axiomatics: Within the deductive calculus, geometric words function purely as uninterpreted placeholders; the sole internal criterion is logical non-contradiction. Yet, as Hoenen demonstrates from Chapter II (§ 9), Hilbert establishes non-contradiction only by mapping geometry back onto intuitive real/algebraic arithmetic ($\Omega$), proving that formal axiomatics cannot be self-grounding without pre-axiomatic intellectual intuition!
